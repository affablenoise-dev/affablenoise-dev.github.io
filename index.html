Workout Playlist Curator

Workout Playlist Curator is a personal-use utility that helps the owner collect workout videos from YouTube into a dedicated private playlist.

The application reads the owner's YouTube subscriptions, channel/video metadata, and playlist contents as needed for discovery and duplicate prevention. It writes selected videos only to the owner's dedicated workout playlist.

The application is not offered as a public service and has no third-party users.
